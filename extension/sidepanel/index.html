Settings & Privacy

Get your free Google AI Studio key →

Without a key, idstack runs in demo mode: every audit shows the same sample findings, whatever the page says, and the page's text is not sent anywhere.

Privacy & Student Data

With a key saved, each audit sends the page's title and text (for a full-course audit, the syllabus and assignments) from your browser to Google's API. On Google's free tier, Google may use that content to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. idstack will not read Canvas grades, Gradebook, SpeedGrader, People, groups, discussions, submissions, or Inbox pages. Other pages can still name students, so audit course materials, not student work.
